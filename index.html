Frequently Asked Questions

What engineering services does POSTI offer in Prince Rupert?

POSTI provides AI Engineering, Data Engineering, Business Automation, Cybersecurity, Cloud Engineering, Software Architecture, DevOps, and Custom Software Development to businesses in Prince Rupert and British Columbia.

Where is POSTI located?

POSTI is headquartered in Prince Rupert, British Columbia, Canada, serving clients across the Northwest and beyond.

What is POSTI's engineering philosophy?

POSTI is an engineering company, not a software company. We apply disciplined engineering to solve business problems — technology is the tool, engineering is the discipline.

What products has POSTI developed?

POSTI's product portfolio includes RestoQuest (restaurant management), CanCheX (quality control), SIMS (inventory management), DownTrack (supply chain), and ReBuy (circular commerce).

Our Philosophy

Engineering Before Technology

POSTI is not a software company. We are an engineering company. Technology is the set of tools we choose — engineering is the discipline that determines how they are applied.

Every project begins with understanding the business problem, not with selecting a framework. Business value always precedes technical elegance.

Simple solutions outperform sophisticated ones. Complexity is introduced only when justified.

Build once, reuse everywhere. Code becomes libraries, libraries become frameworks, frameworks become platforms, platforms become products.

Security by design. Not added at the end — every decision considers authentication, authorization, encryption, and resilience.
